Hi Polar team,

I’m appealing the decision that mybestgame.lol sells “paid ranking boosts or sponsored placements.” I believe that framing misunderstands the product.

## What we sell
mybestgame.lol is **consumer entertainment software**: a live public board of video games. Customers buy a **one-time digital board participation credit**. On payment (`order.paid`), our API instantly credits that game’s listing on our site. Credits stack. Fulfillment is digital and immediate — no physical goods, no human services, no ad network.

## What we do *not* sell
- Not an advertising agency or media buy
- Not lead generation or marketing services
- Not sponsorship inventory sold to brands against an audience guarantee
- No outbound promo links, images, or click tracking on optional notes
- Optional “Game Director Message” is on-site title + text only, and only shows if that buyer is the top contributor for that game — it is not a separately sold ad slot

## Comparable products already live on Polar
Public pay-to-participate boards in the same category already process successfully through Polar (for example **outbid.lol**). We are the same product class: entertainment / attention board with stacked digital credits and instant on-site fulfillment — applied to **video games** instead of startup listings.

## How we describe the Polar product
- Category: consumer software / games
- Product name / label: **Board participation credit**
- Description: one-time digital credit that updates a live on-site game board listing; optional on-site text note (no links); not advertising or sponsorship

Site: https://mybestgame.lol
