# Product Hunt listing

## When to submit

Wait until the board has real activity (multiple games, recent takeovers). A quiet board on launch day kills conversion.

## Tagline (60 chars max)

Live board where fans stack support to climb video games.

## Short description

mybestgame.lol is consumer entertainment software: a live video-game board. Fans buy one-time digital participation credits that stack on a game. Optional Game Director Messages appear on the card when you are the top contributor. Credits last 30 days.

## Maker comment / first comment

Hey Product Hunt —

I built mybestgame.lol as a playful arcade board for games people love. Search a title, stack support (first $1 is free on a device), and optionally leave a short Game Director Message. Only the top contributor’s message shows on the card.

No accounts, no email required for identity — just a handle if you want one. The board only updates after verified payment.

Would love feedback from builders and game fans. What’s the first game you’d put on the board?
